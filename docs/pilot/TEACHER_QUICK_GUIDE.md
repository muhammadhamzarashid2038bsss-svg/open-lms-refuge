# Teacher quick guide

This page is for a teacher using Open LMS Refuge on a shared phone. The words in **bold** are the words on the screen.

## Sign in

1. Open the app.
2. Tap **Staff sign-in**.
3. Type your **Email** and **Password**.
4. Tap **Sign in**.

You then see your teacher home. It says you only see sections assigned to you.

## Find your section

On the teacher home, each section is a line with the section name and the course name. Tap the link that ends in **timetable**.

Learners have a different home page. Theirs has **Today's classes**. Yours uses the timetable.

## Take attendance

1. On the timetable, tap **Take attendance** on the class.
2. For each learner, tap **Present**, **Absent**, **Late**, or **Excused**.
3. For **Absent** or **Excused**, you can add a reason: **Illness**, **Family care**, **Transport or cost**, **Family circumstances (details not recorded)**, or **Unknown**. Tap the reason again to clear it. Select only the reason category. Do not write family details in notes, comments, or other fields.
4. Tap **Save attendance**.

The screen shows **Waiting to send** when marks are still on the phone.

If the screen says **These marks are on this phone, waiting to send. This browser did not promise to keep them.**, the marks were saved locally, but the browser has not promised to protect them from automatic removal. Keep the phone connected and send the marks through **Attendance sync** as soon as possible.

If the screen explicitly says **The marks were not stored**, the attendance changes were not saved. Do not assume they will sync. Resolve the storage problem before trying again.

## Work offline

Open the class once while the phone is online. The screen says **Open this class once while connected** when that step is still needed.

After that, the attendance screen can open without internet, including from the installed app. The installed app opens the attendance screen first. This does not lock the phone or prevent someone from opening other apps.

Marks stay on the phone until the server confirms them. Confirmed marks stay on the phone too.

## Send attendance

When the phone has internet, stay signed in. Look for **Attendance sync**. It shows **Attendance waiting to send**.

Tap **Sync now**. Wait until the waiting number drops, or until the screen says the server confirmed the last send. If it says some marks were refused, do not mark those learners again until someone checks the message.

## Find course materials

On the teacher home, tap **Manage courses and materials**. The next page is **Courses**. Open the course, then the section, to see the materials.

## Grades and notes

On the teacher home you can:

- Tap **Gradebook** on a section line to open that section's marks.
- Tap **Save a grade or note on this phone**. Use **Save grade on this phone** or **Save note on this phone**. Then tap **Send saved edits** when the phone is online.
- Tap **Review grade and note conflicts** if two phones changed the same grade or note. The page title is **Open conflicts**.
- Tap **Performance** to see attendance and grade trends for your section.
- Tap **Handoff reports** to read a transition report that was assigned to you.

Keep notes short. Do not write a learner's legal status, home address, exact birth date, or a sensitive family story.

**Save a practice record on this phone** only stores a practice line with no personal details. It does not mark attendance or save a grade. This is a practice feature, not a way to record learner activity. Do not enter real learner information.

## Shared phones

The box **This phone** says sign-in ends after 30 minutes with no use, or after 8 hours.

Tap **Sign out** before you hand the phone to someone else. Sign-out locks the screen in the app. It does not delete attendance, grades, notes, or homework files saved on the phone. Those records are not encrypted.

Do not leave the phone unlocked on a desk.
